Each tab is its own deck; all decks draw onto the same table. Percentages show each card's chance on the next draw (copies of that card left ÷ cards left in that deck). Drag cards anywhere; double-click a card to return it to its deck. "Reset deck" clears that deck's cards from the table and refills it to the quantities set above. Export saves all quantities, the table layout and card size to a .json file; Import restores them. Click a card thumbnail to replace its image.
Press “Draw card” to pull a random card from the selected deck
